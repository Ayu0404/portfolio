The Situation

NewDay Child Coaching helps parents navigate their child's developmental milestones. My client had real expertise — a valuable digital report to sell, and zero desire to manually chase every lead through the process.

She needed a system that could take a parent from curious visitor to paying customer to delivered product — without any manual intervention.

02
What I Built
  • Scored quiz that segments parents by child development profile
  • Personalised email sequences triggered by quiz results
  • Per-subscriber evergreen countdown (24-hour timed tripwire offer)
  • Dual-price checkout with automatic price escalation on timer expiry
  • Cross-device countdown that persists per subscriber, not per browser
  • Purchase-triggered automatic product delivery — no manual step
  • Branching email logic for new vs returning parent subscribers
  • Full SOP documentation for the entire system
